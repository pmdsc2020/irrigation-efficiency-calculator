3. Calculation method

ETo = [0.408 Δ (Rn − G) + γ × 900/(T + 273) × u₂ × (es − ea)] ÷ [Δ + γ(1 + 0.34u₂)]

Daily calculation assumes G = 0. Wind is converted to 2 m height. Solar radiation, temperature, humidity, latitude, elevation and date are used in the calculation. Slider interpretation bands are indicative learning aids, not official FAO irrigation thresholds.

For learning and preliminary assessment. Use quality-controlled station observations and verify calculations against FAO-56 procedures before design or operational irrigation decisions.
